# Ground floor shops

Every company building has shops on the ground floor. The actual shop counts, numbers, areas and prices have not been supplied. No shop records are fabricated in the company workspace.

Use **Shops → Add shop**, or **Properties → building → Add shop**, to enter the actual shop number, building, ground floor, monthly asking rent, area, amenities and notes. Inventory can be edited and marked under maintenance. Shops are excluded from the Flats list and apartment category filters.

The same tenant can hold flat and shop agreements, with a shared tenant account. Optional company name and trade licence number are stored on that profile. Upload licences to the tenant profile, signed contracts to their agreements, and shop photos/plans to the shop. Originals remain private, scanned and versioned under the existing permissions.

Open a shop to edit it, inspect rental history or create an agreement. Annual agreements use the confirmed four or six cheque workflow, with the other existing installment counts available. The agreed annual rent is entered explicitly; asking prices do not silently update existing agreements. Rent, opening balances, actual cheque clearance and security follow the existing account rules. Cheque reminders include shop rental cheques.

An agreement reserves the shop for its date range. Database row locks reject overlapping tenancies. Shops can only be entered on ground level, and a floor containing shops cannot be renumbered as an upper floor or converted to parking/services. Existing agreement history prevents moving a rental unit to another building/floor or switching its rental type.

Parking remains optional and must belong to the same building as the shop. Reserved lots use the existing allocation safeguards and their charges share the agreement's rent cheques.

Reports offer **Flats & shops**, **Flats only** and **Shops only**. Per-building exports include separate flat/shop counts. Collection filters include payments linked to the selected rental units; account payments without an agreement remain unallocated and are excluded from filtered building/type totals.

VAT and additional commercial fees require the company's confirmed treatment before automatic calculation is implemented. The printable summary remains an unsigned internal draft; the authoritative signed agreement is uploaded separately.
